## Sponsor interpretation

Judges reward **the actual service purchased for a useful job**, not just receipt screenshots. A policy dashboard without a new paid result is substantially weaker than the approved agent procurement thesis. Single cheapest paid call repeated without useful purpose is expressly discouraged. The submission should show one real research task, why the agent chose services, exact cost, useful delivered result, genuine audit receipt and an independent user's benefit.

## Scoring discipline

No numerical simulated score is assigned without an organizer rubric and comparative empirical evidence. Confidence low on market appeal, high on local safety/QAs, low on eligible independent usage.

## Priority order

1. Own agent registration, public repo and official enrollment (eligibility).
2. Full paid API task, bounded payment, authentic result, verified Celo receipt (sponsor's actual magic moment).
3. Independent user and task, user-result evidence.
4. Judge-readable 60–90s final demo and audited submission.
5. Naming/brand/art QA and Product Hygiene; avoid cosmetic work before sponsor P0s.
